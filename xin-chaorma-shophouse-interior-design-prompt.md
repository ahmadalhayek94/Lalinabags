## 0. Instruction Type: IMAGE EDIT, NOT NEW SCENE

Use each uploaded reference photo of the empty shophouse as the **exact architectural base**. Do not redesign, move, enlarge, remove, or invent architecture. Keep, unchanged, from every source photo:

- Exact room dimensions, proportions and floor level

## 1. Design Direction

A sophisticated Mediterranean × Levantine × Vietnamese interior — **not** a generic Middle Eastern restaurant. Premium, contemporary, warm, artistic, highly photogenic.

**Palette**
- Burnt Tangerine `#D64F26`
- Warm Sand `#E4CDA6`
- Deep black (matte, for metal framing and accents)
- Warm off-white / cream plaster
- Natural oak and walnut wood
- Terracotta
- Aged / brushed brass

**Materials**
- Warm textured lime-plaster walls, cream/warm-white
- Natural oak or walnut wood joinery, tables, and chairs
- Terracotta tile used selectively (behind the bread station, as an accent band)
- Handmade-look ceramic tiles for splashes/backsplash
- Brushed brass trim, rails, and signage letterforms
- Black matte metal framing on glass partitions and shelving
- Stone (light travertine-tone) countertops
- Subtle Arabic geometric pattern motifs worked into tile or screen details — restrained, not literal ornamentation
- Subtle Vietnamese lacquer/rattan textures worked in through pendant shades and stools

Keep it clean and restrained — a few strong material moments, not an overdecorated space.

Re-tint and re-material all of this into the Xin Chàorma identity: swap navy/beige for warm sand, burnt tangerine, terracotta and black; swap generic ceramics for Levantine-Vietnamese hybrid pieces (clay jugs, lacquered trays).

**Shawarma station**
- One or two vertical shawarma rotisseries, large premium stainless-steel equipment
- Halal beef/chicken cones rotating vertically
- Warm, dramatic focused lighting highlighting the meat (like a spotlight cone)
- Visible staff prep area behind the counter

**Bread / bánh mì theater**
- Visible artisan bread prep area beside or adjacent to the shawarma station
- Small open bakery / bread oven, warm glow from within
- Fresh Vietnamese bánh mì-style loaves visibly baking or racked
- Subtle nod to Jordanian shrak bread (a domed griddle or flat stone visible in the prep area) worked in as a textural/process detail, not signage

Position the counter so food prep is visually open to the seated dining area — a see-through or partial-height service line, matching where the columns/openings already exist in each source photo.

## 6. Lighting

Warm color temperature, 2700–3000K throughout.
- Warm pendant clusters (per mood reference above) over table groups
- Wall sconces on the accent/feature wall
- Under-counter lighting at the service counter
- Focused accent lighting on the shawarma rotisserie
- Subtle lighting on the brand signage
- Soft architectural cove/uplighting following the existing ceiling geometry

Render realistic shadows, reflections on the stone floor and stainless steel, and a beautiful evening atmosphere that still reads bright enough for service.
